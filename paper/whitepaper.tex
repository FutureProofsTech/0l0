\title{0l0: A Lattice-Concise Post-Quantum Non-Interactive\\ Zero-Knowledge Proof for a Fixed Relation}

\begin{abstract}
We describe a non-interactive zero-knowledge proof of knowledge for a fixed
relation $R^*$ over the cyclotomic ring $R_q = \mathbb{Z}_q[X]/(X^{64}+1)$,
implemented from scratch in safe, dependency-free Rust. Proofs are
$4577$ bytes, proving takes $\approx 190\,\mu\mathrm{s}$ and verification
$\approx 80\,\mu\mathrm{s}$ (p50, x86-64). The construction is a two-round
Lyubashevsky identification scheme compiled with Fiat--Shamir over a BLAKE3
transcript. This paper states the protocol, the exact parameters, three
theorems (completeness, knowledge soundness via extraction, quantified
zero knowledge) with their measured evidence, and the explicitly open
items. It is a prototype specification, not a security audit.
\end{abstract}

\section{Relation and parameters}

\begin{definition}[Relation $R^*$]
Public inputs are an Ajtai matrix $A \in R_q^{4\times 8}$, a commitment
$y = A\cdot w$ and a scalar $t$. A witness is $w \in R_q^8$ with ternary
coefficients such that {\rm (i)} $A\cdot w = y$, {\rm (ii)}
$\lVert w\rVert_\infty \le 1$, $\lVert w\rVert_2 \le 64$, and
{\rm (iii)} $\langle w,w\rangle = t \pmod q$ over centered representatives.
\end{definition}

The modulus admits primitive $64$-th roots of unity, so all ring products
use a from-scratch number-theoretic transform ($W_{64} = 817737403$,
verified of exact order $64$). Responses satisfy
$\lVert z\rVert_\infty \le 65472 < 2^{16}$ and are stored at 17 bits each:
$512 \times 17 = 8704$ bits $= 1088$ bytes exactly, with no padding bits.
A single range check on $[64,131008]$ enforces canonicality and the
response bound together.

\section{Protocol}

Per round $j \in \{0,1\}$ with caller salt $s$:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Sample a uniform mask $r \in [-S,S]^{512}$ from the XOF stream
  keyed by the salt, the round index, and the attempt counter.
\item Commit $t_1 = A\cdot r$ and $u = \langle r,r\rangle \pmod q$.
\item Derive the challenge $c$ (sparse ternary, exactly $16$ nonzero
  $\pm 1$ coefficients) from the transcript over
  $(\mathit{params},A,y,t,s,j,t_1,u)$.
\item Respond $z = r + c\star w$; accept iff $\lVert z\rVert_\infty \le S'$,
  else retry (at most $8$ attempts, else \textsf{SamplingExhausted}).
\end{enumerate}
The proof is
$\mathit{version}\Vert t_{1,0}\Vert z_0\Vert t_{1,1}\Vert z_1\Vert
u_0\,0\,u_1\,0\Vert s$ ($4577$ bytes, version $2$). The verifier
re-derives both challenges and checks, per round,
$A\cdot z = t_1 + c\star y$, the response bound, decoding canonicality,
and reserved-zero regions.

The challenge space per round is $\binom{64}{16}\cdot 2^{16} = 2^{64.80}$,
giving forking error $\approx 2^{-129.6}$ over two rounds.

\section{Theorems}

\begin{theorem}[Completeness]
An honest prover outputs an accepting proof with probability at least
$1 - 1.3\times 10^{-3}$ per proof (else \textsf{SamplingExhausted}, and
the caller retries with fresh salt).
\end{theorem}
\noindent\textit{Sketch.} $A\cdot z = A\cdot r + c\star A\cdot w = t_1 +
c\star y$ by $R_q$-linearity. Since $\lVert c\star w\rVert_\infty \le 16$,
per-coefficient acceptance is $(2S'+1)/(2S+1)$ and independence gives
per-round accept $\approx 0.606$ (measured $14$--$34/40$); two rounds with
$8$ retries fail with probability $\approx 1.3\times 10^{-3}$ (measured
$\le 2/60$). \hfill $\square$

\begin{theorem}[Knowledge soundness]
From two accepting transcripts sharing $(t_1,u)$ with distinct challenges,
either the extractor recovers $w^*$ satisfying all three claims of $R^*$
exactly, or the challenge difference vanishes (discarded; usability
$\approx 1-64/q$ per pair, measured $0/200$ non-invertible). Forgery
without extraction needs transcript grinding at $\approx 2^{-130}$ (ROM),
each attempt additionally facing a $q^{-256}$ direct-equation barrier.
\end{theorem}
\noindent\textit{Sketch.} Same mask $r$ at the fork gives
$\Delta z = \Delta c\star w$; invertible $\Delta c$ yields
$w^* = \Delta c^{-1}\star\Delta z = w$ exactly, and all claims are checked
on $w^*$ directly — no slack bound is needed. No verifier equation can pin
$\langle w,w\rangle = t$ on its own (kernel-ambiguity argument); packing
closes through extraction, exhibited at $488/488$ on a tiny model with
all three claims checked. \hfill $\square$

\begin{theorem}[Zero knowledge, quantified]
Accepted responses are exactly uniform on $[-S',S']^{512}$.
For fixed shift $|s| \le S-S'$, acceptance keeps precisely the values
mapping bijectively onto the response range, so the conditional distance
is zero in total variation (exhibited by a $\chi^2$ test).
Mask commitments are
functions of uniform randomness only. Computational hiding rests on mask
recovery, estimated at Core-SVP $2^{38}$--$2^{42}$ (marginal — not claimed
as production-grade).
\end{theorem}
\noindent\textit{Sketch.} The uniformity claim is per-coefficient exact
arithmetic, lifted jointly by independence. A former packing scalar
$v = \langle r,c\star w\rangle$ was removed from the wire after variance
analysis showed it encodes $\lVert cw\rVert_2$ ($w=0$ forces $v\equiv0$);
its slots are canonically zero and enforced. \hfill $\square$

\section{Measured evidence (release day, x86-64)}

75/75 tests green (dev and \texttt{--release}); 11/11 official BLAKE3
KATs; an independent Python model produces byte-identical proofs (2/2);
a tiny-model extractor recovers witnesses on all 488 usable challenge
pairs with 0/2400 direct grinds; exhaustive single-bitflip sweep
36,616/36,616 reject; 20,000-trial random-wire grinding game at 0 accepts;
`cargo-fuzz` 14.9M runs clean; full Miri lib run 35/35 green; prover
p50 $184\,\mu\mathrm{s}$, verifier p50 $80\,\mu\mathrm{s}$.

\section{Open items (exact statements)}

\begin{enumerate}
\item Tail bound on $\lVert \Delta c^{-1}\rVert_1$: closed by analysis
  (the extractor verifies directly, so no tail bound is needed).
\item Closed-form forking-reduction constants: structure proven,
  constants explicitly out of prototype scope.
\item Hiding beyond conditional uniformity: superseded (responses are
  exactly uniform); computational leg marginal by measurement.
\item Gaussian sampling, BKZ-simulation depth, external audit, lab side
  channels, NIST parameter blessing: roadmap or out-of-scope items,
  tracked in \texttt{ROADMAP.md}.
\end{enumerate}
