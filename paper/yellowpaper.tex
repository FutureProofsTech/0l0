\begin{abstract}
This document normatively specifies the \texttt{0l0} v0.3.0 proof
system: symbols, domains, data structures with exact byte layouts,
deterministic algorithms for proving and verification, the Fiat--Shamir
transcript schedule, and test vectors. It is the companion to the
whitepaper (protocol and theorems); in case of divergence between prose
and code, the test suite decides, and this paper is amended.
\end{abstract}

\section{Data structures}

Polynomials are $64$ coefficients in $[0,q)$, coefficient-major,
little-endian 32-bit words unless packed. A \emph{centered} value is
$c - q$ if $c > q/2$ else $c$.

\section{Algorithms}

\begin{definition}[Commit]
$t = A\cdot w$ with negacyclic products via negative wrapped convolution:
twist by $\psi^j$, size-64 NTT, pointwise multiply, inverse NTT, untwist.
Normative result equals schoolbook $O(D^2)$ multiplication bitwise.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Prove, per round $j$ with salt $s$]
For $\mathit{attempt} \in \{0,\dots,7\}$: seed
$\gets H(s\Vert j\Vert\mathit{attempt})$; sample mask $r$ uniform on
$[-S,S]^{512}$ by rejection from the XOF stream; $t_1 \gets A\cdot r$;
$u \gets \langle r,r\rangle$. Let $h$ be the transcript output of
Section~4; the challenge $c \gets \texttt{Expand}(h)$
is sparse ternary with exactly $16$ nonzero $\pm 1$ entries.
Respond $z \gets r + c\star w$; accept iff
$\lVert z\rVert_\infty \le S'$.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Verify]
Parse version ($=2$), enforce zero regions and packed-word ranges, then per
round re-derive $c$ and check $A\cdot z = t_1 + c\star y$ and
$\lVert z\rVert_\infty \le S'$. Reject on any failure
(\textsf{MalformedEncoding} for encoding, \textsf{VerificationFailed} else).
\end{definition}

\section{Transcript schedule}

\texttt{FS} absorbs, in order: \texttt{params} ($D,q,\beta,B,S',\tau$,
version), \texttt{A} ($H$ of the flattened matrix), \texttt{y} (1024 B),
\texttt{t}, \texttt{salt}, \texttt{round}, \texttt{t1} (1024 B),
\texttt{u}. Absorption is $H(\mathit{state}\Vert\mathit{label}\Vert
\mathit{len}_{16}\Vert\mathit{data})$ (data over 1024 B is digested first);
labels $\le 64$ B, data $\le 2048$ B. Challenges squeeze 32 B under tag
$0x50$. The hash is BLAKE3-32 (spec-exact) with a documented
counter-cascade XOF, not the official seek mode.
